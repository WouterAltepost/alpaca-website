Privacy statement

The short version: we only collect what we need to talk to you, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Last updated 7 September 2026.

Who we are

Alpaca AI, Vrijheidslaan 18, 1078 PJ Amsterdam, the Netherlands. KVK 42024441, VAT id NL005436235B79. We are the controller for the personal data described here. Questions about your data go to wout@alpacaai.nl.

What we collect, and why

  • When you book a discovery call: your name, email address and the time you pick, plus your company name and what you want to discuss if you fill those in. We use this to plan and hold the call and to send you the confirmation. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • When you email or call us: whatever you share with us, which we use to answer you and to keep track of the conversation. Legal basis: the same, or our legitimate interest in keeping a record of what was discussed.
  • When you visit the site: our hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, for security and to keep the site running. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.
  • Statistics: we use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without storing personal data. It records the page, the referrer, the country and the device type, all in aggregate. We cannot see who you are.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the font file is requested.

Who else sees your data

A few service providers help us run the site and the booking flow. They process data on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for anything else:

  • Vercel, hosting of the website and visitor statistics.
  • Supabase, the database that stores bookings.
  • n8n, the automation that handles a booking and sends the emails.
  • Google Workspace, email.

Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, it does so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Booking details: 12 months after the call, unless we start working together, in which case they become part of our client records.
  • Email: as long as needed to handle the conversation, and afterwards for as long as the law requires us to keep it.
  • Server logs: kept by the hosting provider for a short period. Statistics contain no personal data.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, delete or hand over the data we hold about you, and to limit or object to how we use it. Email wout@alpacaai.nl and we respond within a month. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

We update this statement when the site or the way we work changes. The date at the top tells you when it was last revised.
