About us

AI does not have to be hard.

Most companies see AI as complicated, risky and expensive. We started Alpaca AI to show it does not have to be.

Why we exist

Engineers first.
Consultants second.

We are engineers who have watched too many AI pilots stall: a demo impresses the board, and six months later nothing runs in production.

That is why Alpaca AI exists. We scope the problem, build the system and get it running inside your business, usually within weeks. The people you talk to are the people building your product.

AI is made to look harder than it is. Done properly, AI is ordinary engineering: a clear scope, a clear price and a result you can measure. It does not take a big budget to make a big difference, and for most companies it is one of the best investments they can make right now.

Principles

Three things we hold to

01

Built around your business, not the model.

We start from your workflow and your data, and choose the technology last. The model is a component, not the point.

02

Production over demos.

If it does not run in your business, it does not count. We ship working systems, not proofs of concept.

03

We say no when AI is the wrong tool.

Some problems need a spreadsheet, a rule or a hire. You get an honest answer, not a pitch.

The team

Small team.
No layers.

A small team of engineers and data specialists who build the systems themselves. No account managers, no handovers, no layers between you and the people doing the work. Based in Amsterdam.
